❓ Help & Tips

How the four modes work
💰 Cost — Enter start and target %. Get cost, time, and range.
💵 Money — Enter how much to spend. Get % reached, kWh, and time. (Public only)
⏱️ Time — Enter start and target %. Get how long it takes.
⏳ Duration — Enter hours + minutes, or a target clock time. Get % reached and cost.
Connector types
CCS2 — DC fast + AC combined. Common on newer EVs.
CHAdeMO — DC fast. Older Japanese EVs (Leaf).
Type 2 — AC only. Does not imply DC capability.
GB/T — Chinese vehicles. AC and DC variants are distinct.
Data quality note

Vehicle specs and station data come from multiple sources, including community reports. Battery capacities may be gross rather than usable. Range cycles differ (NEDC / WLTP / CLTC) — never compare them as equal. Always verify the exact rate at the charger before plugging in.

Range cycles
NEDC — Oldest standard. Usually the highest (most optimistic) number.
WLTP — Newer, more realistic than NEDC.
CLTC — Chinese standard. Typically between NEDC and WLTP.
Real world — Expect 15–25% less than rated in Sri Lankan heat.
