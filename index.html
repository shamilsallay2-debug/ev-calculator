❓ Help & About

What this app does

This app helps Sri Lankan EV owners figure out how much a charge will cost, how long it will take, and how far you can drive afterwards — before you plug in. It supports all major local charging networks plus home charging with your domestic tariff.

You can add your own vehicles to your garage with custom specs, and the app will use them for all calculations.

How to use it — step by step
1️⃣
Pick your car — tap the vehicle name on the Home page. Use "My Garage" for custom vehicles, or pick from the built-in list.
2️⃣
Set your charge range — drag the sliders or use a preset like "20→80 Daily."
3️⃣
Choose where you're charging — tap 🏠 Home or 🔌 Public DC, and pick the network.
4️⃣
Read the result — cost, time, and range are shown at the top. Tap "Compare with other options" to see network and petrol comparisons.
5️⃣
Save it — tap "💾 Save this charge" to log it. Find it under 📊 History.
🚗
Add your car — go to the 🚗 Garage tab and tap "➕ Add" to save your vehicle with its exact specs.
🔌
Report a station — on the Stations tab, tap a status (Working/Slow/Broken/Blocked) to help other drivers know what to expect.
Quick tips
⚡
Above 80% is slow. DC charging tapers off dramatically past 80%. For daily driving, stop at 80%.
💰
Home is cheaper. Home charging is typically 3–5× cheaper than public DC.
🌡️
Heat affects range. In Sri Lanka, expect 15–25% less range than the manufacturer rating.
⛽
Update fuel prices. Set current petrol/diesel prices in Settings so the petrol comparison stays accurate.
The math behind it

Energy you'll add:

kWh = (Target% − Current%) ÷ 100 × Capacity

What you'll pay for:

Billed = Needed ÷ (Efficiency ÷ 100)

Total cost:

Cost = (Billed × Rate) + Tax + Session Fee

Petrol comparison:

Distance = Range × (Target% ÷ 100)
Petrol cost = Distance ÷ km/L × fuel price
Rate disclaimer

Network rates are based on published tariffs and may change. Always verify the rate shown at the charger. The calculator lets you enter any custom rate.
