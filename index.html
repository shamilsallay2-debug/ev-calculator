❓ Help & About

What this app does

This app helps Sri Lankan EV owners figure out how much a charge will cost, how long it will take, and how far you can drive afterwards — before you plug in. It supports all major local charging networks plus home charging with your domestic tariff.

All calculations happen instantly as you move the sliders. Your charging history syncs to your account so you can track costs over time and access it from any device.

How to use it — step by step
1️⃣
Pick your car — tap the vehicle name at the top and choose from the list. Your battery size sets everything else automatically.
2️⃣
Set your charge range — drag the sliders to match where your battery is now and where you want it to be. Or use a preset like "20→80 Daily."
3️⃣
Choose where you're charging — tap 🏠 Home for your domestic rate, or 🔌 Public DC for a station. Pick the network from the dropdown.
4️⃣
Read the result — the top card shows cost, time, and range. Below that, compare with other networks.
5️⃣
Save it — tap "💾 Save this charge" to log it. Find it later under 📊 History.
💡
Advanced stuff (session fees, tax, efficiency) is hidden under "Advanced options" — you can ignore it unless you need it.
Quick tips
⚡
Above 80% is slow. DC charging tapers off dramatically past 80%. For daily driving, stop at 80%.
💰
Home is cheaper. Home charging is typically 3–5× cheaper than public DC — but slower.
🌡️
Heat affects range. In Sri Lanka's climate, expect 15–25% less range than the manufacturer rating.
📊
Track your costs. Over a few months you'll see exactly what you spend on charging — useful for tax or budgeting.
The math behind it

Energy you'll add:

kWh = (Target% − Current%) ÷ 100 × Capacity

What you'll pay for:

Billed = Needed ÷ (Efficiency ÷ 100)

Total cost:

Cost = (Billed × Rate) + Tax + Session Fee

Charging time (rough):

Time (h) = Needed ÷ Charger Power
Rate disclaimer

Network rates are based on published tariffs and may change. Always verify the rate shown at the charger. The calculator lets you enter any custom rate — use that if the station price differs.
