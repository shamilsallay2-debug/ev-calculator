❓ Help & About

What this app does

This app helps Sri Lankan EV owners figure out how much a charge will cost, how long it will take, and how far you can drive afterwards — before you plug in. It supports all major local charging networks plus home charging with your domestic tariff.

You can add your own vehicles to your garage with custom specs, and the app will use them for all calculations.

How to use it — step by step
1️⃣
Pick your car — tap the vehicle name on the Home page. Your chosen vehicle is remembered for next time.
2️⃣
Set your charge range — drag the sliders or tap the number to type directly.
3️⃣
Choose where you're charging — tap 🏠 Home or 🔌 Public DC. Your last-used rate is remembered.
4️⃣
Read the result — cost, time, and range. Tap "Compare" for network and petrol comparisons.
5️⃣
Save it — tap "💾 Save this charge" to log it. View savings in 📊 History.
📍
Find nearby chargers — on the 🔌 Stations tab, tap "Find Stations Near Me."
➕
Submit a missing charger — on the Stations tab, tap "Submit a Station" and share its GPS location.
🔌
Report a station — tap Working / Slow / Broken / Blocked to help other drivers.
ⓘ
Need help with a field? — tap any small "i" icon next to a label.
Quick tips
⚡
Above 80% is slow. DC charging tapers off dramatically past 80%.
💰
Home is cheaper. Typically 3–5× cheaper than public DC.
🌡️
Heat affects range. Expect 15–25% less than the rated range.
⛽
Update fuel prices. Set current prices in Settings for accurate petrol comparison.
The math behind it

Energy you'll add:

kWh = (Target% − Current%) ÷ 100 × Capacity

What you'll pay for:

Billed = Needed ÷ (Efficiency ÷ 100)

Total cost:

Cost = (Billed × Rate) + Tax + Session Fee

Petrol comparison:

Distance = Range × (Target% ÷ 100)
Petrol cost = Distance ÷ km/L × fuel price
Rate disclaimer

Network rates are based on published tariffs and may change. Always verify the rate shown at the charger.
