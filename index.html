❓ Help & Tips

How the four modes work
💰 Cost — Enter start and target %. Get cost, time, and range.
💵 Money — Enter how much to spend. Get % reached, kWh, and time.
⏱️ Time — Enter start and target %. Get how long it takes.
⏳ Duration — Enter hours + minutes, or a target clock time. Get % reached and cost.
Quick tips
⚡ Above 80% is slow — DC charging tapers off dramatically.
💰 Home is cheaper — Typically 3–5× cheaper than public DC.
🌡️ Heat affects range — Expect 15–25% less than rated.
✏️ Edit history — Tap any saved charge to fix values.
