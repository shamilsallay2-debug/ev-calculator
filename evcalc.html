❓ Help

Public Charging

Energy to Add:

kWh = (Target% − Current%) ÷ 100 × Capacity

Billed Energy: Adds charging losses.

Billed = Needed ÷ (Efficiency ÷ 100)

Total Cost:

Cost = (Billed × Rate) + Tax + Session Fee
Home Charging

Home charging uses AC power, which is slower but far cheaper per kWh. The formulas are the same, but efficiency is slightly lower (AC losses) and there's no session fee.

Charging Time:

Time (h) = Energy Needed ÷ Charger Power

Cost per km: Total cost ÷ range gained. Useful for comparing home vs. public charging in daily use.

☀️ Solar Power (Home)

If you have rooftop solar, the calculator estimates how much of your charging energy comes from solar (free) vs. the grid (paid).

Current Sri Lankan policy: Under the Net Plus scheme (for new connections after March 2026), solar generation is sold to the grid and household consumption is billed separately. Solar only reduces your charging cost if you charge while the system is producing and self-consume the energy.

How to estimate the Daytime Charging Share:

  • Overnight charging = 0%
  • Plugging in at 9 AM on a sunny day = 70–90%
  • Plugging in at 4 PM = 30–50%
  • Plugging in at 7 PM = 0%
Efficiency Guide

DC Fast: 90–99% efficient

AC Home: 85–92% efficient

Losses mean the station or meter bills you for slightly more kWh than your battery stores.
